UPWARD — EDITABLE 3D BUILDINGS

Files:
- upward-editable-buildings.obj: all exterior shells and interior rooms, with separately named wall pieces.
- upward-editable-buildings.mtl: matching materials. Keep beside the OBJ when importing.

Import the OBJ into Blender (File > Import > Wavefront OBJ), then save as a .blend if desired. Each box/wall is a separate named mesh. Object/group names ending in _EXTERIOR or _INTERIOR let you select, hide, or edit a complete building side.

Axes and dimensions match the game: Y is up; units are meters. The six original locations and three unlockable destinations are included at their in-game coordinates. Street-facing entrances are on +X for buildings on the left side of the road and -X for buildings on the right. Door panels are separate objects from their wall sections. Exterior and interior meshes share coordinates because the game swaps between them; hide the opposite group while editing to avoid visual overlap.
